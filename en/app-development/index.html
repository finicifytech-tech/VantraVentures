An app development company with one team, end to end

Building an app is more than designing screens. Backend, database, user management, payments, notifications, admin panel and app store release are all parts of the same product. Splitting them across vendors costs time and clarity, so as an app development company in Istanbul we keep them under one roof with one point of contact.

For iOS and Android we often use React Native, which lets one codebase serve both platforms. It shortens development and lets updates ship to both stores together. We choose the stack with you, based on what the product actually needs.

We work in English and Turkish, with Turkish startups and with foreign founders and companies building products for the Turkish market or running their tech from Istanbul.

Process

How we work.

  1. Idea and scope

    We talk through the problem, your users and the must-have features, and trim the first version where it makes sense.

  2. Proposal and roadmap

    Scope, timeline and price are agreed in writing, with clear phases and delivery points.

  3. Design and prototype

    We design user flows and screens, and move to development once you approve them.

  4. Build and test

    We build in stages and share a test build with you at each one.

  5. Launch and grow

    We handle the store release and keep improving the product based on real user feedback.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to develop an app?

It depends entirely on scope. A focused MVP and a multi-role platform with payments and AI features are very different projects. Once scope is clear, we give you a written timeline with the proposal.

How much does app development cost?

We don't have fixed packages. After hearing your idea and requirements, we prepare a project-based quote that reflects the features, platforms and integrations involved.

Do you build separate apps for iOS and Android?

In most projects we use React Native, so one codebase serves both platforms. That saves time and makes maintenance simpler. Where a product calls for a different approach, we discuss it with you.

I'm a foreign founder. Can we work in English?

Yes. We work in English and Turkish, and can build apps and admin panels in both languages, or more, from the start.

Can you add AI features to my app?

Yes. We build chatbots, LLM integrations, summarisation, automated reporting and workflow automation. Mentora and Finicify Terminal are live products of ours that include AI features.

Do you support the app after launch?

Yes. Free support is included after delivery. After that, you can choose a monthly plan for maintenance, updates and new features.
