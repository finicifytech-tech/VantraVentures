FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a website take?

A business website usually takes 2-4 weeks. Larger projects and apps take longer depending on scope. We give you a clear timeline with the proposal.

How much does it cost?

Every business needs something different, so we do not sell fixed packages. After the free audit we send a clear, project-based quote.

When does SEO start working?

Technical fixes start to show within a few weeks. Lasting rankings for competitive searches usually take 3-6 months of consistent work.

Who owns the site and the code?

You do. The domain, hosting, source code, Google Analytics, Search Console and ad accounts are set up in your business name and handed over.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a free support period after launch. After that you can choose one of our monthly plans for maintenance, updates and content.
